### 🎯 The problem

One business failure in four comes from late payment. The classic answer is pressure — more calls,
harder letters, a customer relationship spent to recover a single invoice. It works badly and it
costs twice.

### 💡 How we solve it

A debtor who can **see what they owe**, **pick a plan that fits**, and **answer on the channel they
already use** pays without anyone having to threaten them. So we automate the patient part and keep
humans for the part that actually needs judgment.

| | |
|---|---|
| 📨 **[Relancer](https://aistos.fr/relancer)** | White-label reminders under *your* brand — five channels (postal mail, AI voice, human call, SMS, email), sequenced and prioritised automatically, with dispute detection. |
| 🤝 **[Recouvrer](https://aistos.fr/recouvrement-externalise)** | Outsourced collection, amicable through judicial, run by people who do this for a living. |
| 📱 **[Debtor portal](https://aistos.fr/portail)** | Mobile-first, no account to create. Payment plans and [direct open-banking payment](https://aistos.fr/solutions-paiement). |

The two compose: start outsourced, bring it in-house as you grow — same platform underneath.

**Who this is for:** banks, fintechs, BNPL and telecom operators, utilities, insurers, social
landlords, healthcare — anyone carrying receivables at volume.
